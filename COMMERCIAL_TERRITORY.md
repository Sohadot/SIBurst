# Commercial Territory

This document is a public map of application areas for the SIBurst identity.
It describes where the concept naturally applies. It is not a business plan,
and it names no organizations.

## The organizing question

> Different systems. One underlying question:
> what changes when capability crosses into a different operating regime?

Every territory below is a different relationship to that question. The four
functions follow the structure of the thesis in `FOUNDATION_THESIS.md`: capability
must be built, it acts, it is measured, and at some point it may cross.

## The four functions

### BUILD — the capacity that accumulates

- compute
- training infrastructure
- inference
- orchestration

Build is where capability accumulates. Its relevance to SIBurst lies in the
point at which infrastructure must be redesigned rather than scaled, because
the systems it serves now operate differently.

### ACT — capability that operates in environments

- agents
- autonomous systems
- embodied systems
- machine action

Act is where capability stops producing outputs for someone and starts taking
actions within an environment. That shift is one of the clearest candidate
thresholds in the framework.

### MEASURE — knowing which regime a system is in

- evaluation
- observability
- capability monitoring
- transition detection

Measure asks not only "how well does it perform?" but "has what it can do
changed in kind?" Transition detection is the function most specific to SIBurst:
it exists only if regimes can change.

### CROSS — the transition itself

- thresholds
- regime transition
- deployment into a new operational class

Cross is the territory the name describes most directly. Here the subject is
not a component but the crossing: preparing for it, recognizing it, and
governing what follows it.

## Forms of identity

SIBurst can function as any of the following. They are listed, not ranked.

| Form | What the name would identify |
|---|---|
| **Company identity** | An organization whose purpose is organized around capability transitions. |
| **Platform identity** | A system on which others build, run, or observe capability that may change regime. |
| **Infrastructure identity** | A layer — compute, orchestration, monitoring — designed for systems before and after a threshold. |
| **Research-program identity** | A body of inquiry into thresholds, discontinuity, and regime change in intelligence. |
| **Category identity** | A name for the category of work concerned with the transition itself. |

## Relationship to the boundary

These territories are natural application areas, not limits on the identity
(see `DECISION_LOG.md`, DEC-008). Any use inside or outside them is still
subject to the boundary test in `CATEGORY_BOUNDARY.md`, and any public claim
made under the name is subject to `PUBLIC_CLAIM_POLICY.md`.
