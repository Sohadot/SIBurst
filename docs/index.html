SIBurst Naming the phase change in intelligence.

SIBurst names a phase change in intelligence: the transition from incremental capability growth into a materially different operating regime.

SIBurst does not assert that superintelligence has been achieved, nor does it predict when or whether a specific transition will occur.

S0 QUIET ACCUMULATION

DEMONSTRATION STATE · S0 / ACCUMULATING

A system accumulates inside fixed rules.

Demonstration stage 1 of 7: accumulating. 3 entities sit in a lattice of 4 lanes and 6 cells per lane. Position follows arrival order, and each of the 2 relationships is drawn along the rails.

S1 SCALE

DEMONSTRATION STATE · S1 / DENSITY RISING

More entities. More relationships. The same rules.

Demonstration stage 2 of 7: density rising. 15 entities and 17 relationships occupy the same lattice under the same rules. Every relationship can still be routed along the rails.

S2 THRESHOLD

DEMONSTRATION STATE · S2 / THRESHOLD APPROACHING

Relationships begin to exceed the structure built to hold them.

Demonstration stage 3 of 7: threshold approaching. 22 entities nearly fill the lattice. 7 of the 29 relationships cannot be routed; each is shown as a stub that stops at the edge of its cell with a short cross mark. Every entity is still centred in its cell.

S3 INSTABILITY

DEMONSTRATION STATE · S3 / REGIME UNSTABLE

The old description fails in places.

Demonstration stage 4 of 7: regime unstable. All 24 entities are present. 17 of the 41 relationships cannot be routed, and entities with unrouted relationships have slipped from the centres of their cells toward them. The rails are unchanged and the lattice rules still apply.

S4 PHASE CHANGE

DEMONSTRATION STATE · S4 / TRANSITION DETECTED

SYSTEM DEMONSTRATION — a model of the SIBurst concept, not a measurement.

The rules change. The entities remain.

Demonstration stage 5 of 7: transition detected. The rails retire. The same 24 entities move from lattice positions to positions set by their relationships, and every stub resolves into its full relationship. No entity or relationship is added or removed. Anchors N08 and N09, neighbours in the lattice, separate; anchors N05 and N19, at opposite corners of the lattice, come together.

S5 SIBURST

DEMONSTRATION STATE · S5 / SIBURST

SIBurst

SIBurst names this crossing: from more capability to a different operating regime.

Demonstration stage 6 of 7: SIBurst. The field is stable. The transition record compares the end of the lattice with the field: the same 24 entities and the same anchors, organized by different rules.

S6 THE AFTER

DEMONSTRATION STATE · S6 / NEW OPERATING REGIME

SIBurst names the transition, not the prediction.

Demonstration stage 7 of 7: new operating regime. The field recedes and the reference layer opens. The demonstration ends here and does not restart.
